## fig-01-chunk-state.png

插入位置：7.2 状态机 ASCII 图之后
画面意图：chunked 请求有独立"住所"，与 waiting/running 三足鼎立。

```
STYLE: Flat vector technical diagram for an engineering textbook, light background #FAFAFA,
uniform 2px strokes, rounded rectangles, orthogonal connectors, no gradients,
no drop shadows, no 3D, no skeuomorphism, no human figures, no decorative patterns,
generous whitespace, sans-serif labels, crisp high-contrast lines, 16:9, 2x resolution.

Three rounded containers in a horizontal row, evenly spaced:
left labeled "WAITING QUEUE", center labeled "RUNNING BATCH", right labeled "CHUNKED".

Arrows:
1. Thick blue arrow from WAITING to RUNNING labeled "ADMIT".
2. Blue arrow from RUNNING to CHUNKED labeled "CUT".
3. Thick blue self-loop on CHUNKED, drawn above the box, labeled "NEXT CHUNK".
4. Green arrow from CHUNKED to RUNNING labeled "LAST CHUNK".
5. Gray dashed arrow from CHUNKED back to WAITING, routed below everything, labeled
   "PARKED".

Inside the CHUNKED box, draw three small horizontal segments stacked, with the middle
one filled solid orange and labeled "PREFIX", to indicate a partially computed prompt.

Add a small orange tag under the CHUNKED box: "AT MOST ONE".
```

标签清单：`WAITING QUEUE` `RUNNING BATCH` `CHUNKED` `ADMIT` `CUT` `NEXT CHUNK` `LAST CHUNK` `PARKED` `PREFIX` `AT MOST ONE`
